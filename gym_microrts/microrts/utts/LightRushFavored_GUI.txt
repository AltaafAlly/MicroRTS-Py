LightRushFavored_GUI.json
=========================
Purpose: symmetric UTT for GUI / manual tests where Light units are much stronger
than TestUnitTypeTable — lightRushAI spams Lights; workerRushAI mostly uses Workers,
so this favors the light-rush playstyle more than the worker-rush one.

Relative to TestUnitTypeTable.json (Light only):
  cost 2→1, hp 4→30, minDamage 2→8, maxDamage 2→14, produceTime 65→35,
  moveTime 8→2, attackTime 5→2, sightRadius 2→3.

Load in MicroRTS GUI:
  Point the client at this file under microrts/utts/ (path depends on your launcher).

From repo root (headless sanity check exists via run_match_configured / GA):
  utts/LightRushFavored_GUI.json

Pair with e.g. maps/10x10/basesWorkers10x10.xml, AIs lightRushAI vs workerRushAI.
